# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview
This directory contains the complete OctoAcme project management framework — a structured approach designed to help teams deliver projects efficiently with clear processes, defined roles, and consistent communication.

### Core Principles
- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named PM and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning

## Brief Project Management Processes Summary
OctoAcme follows a six-phase lifecycle: Initiation, Planning, Execution & Tracking, Release & Deployment, Retrospective & Continuous Improvement, and Risk Management & Communication. Initiation validates the business need and aligns stakeholders using a one-pager that captures goals and success metrics. Planning turns approved initiatives into prioritized backlogs with estimates, a Definition of Done, and a release plan that maps milestones and dependencies.

Execution uses a project board workflow (Backlog → Ready → In Progress → In Review → QA → Done) and emphasizes small, testable PRs with clear acceptance criteria. Communication cadence includes daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, demos at the end of sprints/milestones, and monthly stakeholder updates. Quality practices require automated CI (tests, linters, security scanning), unit/integration tests, and manual QA where needed. Releases follow pre-release checks, staging verification, rollback plans, and post-deploy validations.

Retrospectives are timeboxed and prioritize 2–3 action items which are tracked in the backlog; action items are reviewed in weekly PM syncs. Risk management is maintained through a Risk Register capturing impact, likelihood, owner, and mitigation plans, and escalations follow defined paths from team-level to sponsor. Together, these processes aim to make project execution repeatable, measurable, and discoverable across the organization.
