# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance Lead defines and oversees testing strategies, manages QA resources, and ensures quality standards are consistently met throughout the project lifecycle. They work closely with the development team and project management to establish quality benchmarks and prevent defects.

### Responsibilities
- Define and implement comprehensive testing strategies aligned with project scope
- Manage QA team resources and workload planning
- Create and maintain test plans, test cases, and quality documentation
- Coordinate testing activities across development and deployment phases
- Track quality metrics and defect trends
- Identify quality risks and recommend mitigation strategies
- Conduct test reviews and quality assessments before release

### Goals
- Ensure all deliverables meet defined quality standards
- Minimize defects in production environments
- Provide early detection of quality issues to reduce rework costs
- Build team confidence in product quality

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Collaborates on test strategy and acceptance criteria, reviews code quality concerns
- **With Project Manager**: Reports quality metrics, flags quality-related risks and dependencies, participates in planning to ensure adequate testing time
- **With Execution Lead**: Coordinates testing schedules and resource allocation
- **With Stakeholders**: Provides quality status reports and release readiness assessments

### Typical Communication
- Quality status in sprint reviews and project status meetings
- Defect reports and trend analysis
- Test plan reviews and quality gate approvals
- Risk escalations related to quality concerns

---

## Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
The Compliance Officer ensures that all project deliverables, processes, and decisions align with regulatory requirements, organizational policies, and industry standards. They provide guidance on compliance requirements and help the team navigate complex regulatory landscapes.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and document regulatory and policy requirements applicable to the project
- Review project plans, designs, and deliverables for compliance
- Conduct compliance audits and assessments
- Provide guidance on compliance-related trade-offs and risk mitigation
- Maintain compliance documentation and audit trails
- Escalate compliance risks and violations
- Coordinate with legal and risk management as needed
- Ensure compliance gates are included in the project workflow

### Goals
- Prevent regulatory violations and organizational policy breaches
- Reduce legal and reputational risk
- Ensure sustainable, compliant business practices
- Build stakeholder confidence in project integrity

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Manager**: Participates in risk assessments, defines compliance gates in the project plan, reviews project decisions
- **With Developers**: Reviews technical implementations for compliance implications, provides guidance on data handling and security
- **With Execution Lead**: Ensures compliance checkpoints are included in release processes
- **With Stakeholders**: Provides compliance assurance and documents compliance status

### Typical Communication
- Compliance requirement briefings during planning
- Compliance review gates and sign-offs
- Risk registers and compliance findings
- Audit documentation and compliance status reports

---

## Technical Architecture Advisor

### Role Summary
The Technical Architecture Advisor provides expert guidance on system design, technology choices, and technical strategy. They help the team make sound architectural decisions that support scalability, maintainability, and long-term technical health.

### Responsibilities
- Review and provide guidance on system architecture and design decisions
- Evaluate technology options and recommend appropriate solutions
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers on architectural best practices
- Ensure alignment with organizational technology standards
- Participate in design reviews and technical discussions
- Help balance technical excellence with delivery timelines and business needs

### Goals
- Ensure sound, scalable technical architecture
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable team capability growth
- Support long-term product maintainability

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Manager**: Supports during planning phase, flags technical dependencies and timeline impacts
- **With Developers**: Reviews technical decisions, provides mentoring and guidance
- **With Product Manager**: Discusses trade-offs between features and technical feasibility
- **With Quality Assurance Lead**: Discusses testability and quality implications of architecture

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review meetings and design discussions
- Technical documentation and decision records
- Risk identification and mitigation planning
- Guidance and mentoring in code reviews and planning sessions

---

## Stakeholder Communications Manager

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Communications Manager develops and executes comprehensive communication strategies to keep stakeholders informed, engaged, and aligned throughout the project lifecycle. They ensure consistent messaging and facilitate effective information flow between project teams and key stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Develop stakeholder communication plans and strategies
- Create and deliver regular status updates and reports for stakeholders
- Manage stakeholder expectations and address concerns
- Facilitate stakeholder engagement and feedback sessions
- Coordinate communication across project teams
- Maintain stakeholder communication documentation and records
- Escalate critical issues or changes to appropriate stakeholders

### Goals
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and engagement
- Prevent miscommunication and misaligned expectations
- Build stakeholder confidence in project execution
- Ensure transparent and timely information flow

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Manager**: Reports to Project Manager, receives guidance on priorities and messaging
- **With Team Leads**: Coordinates with technical, product, and delivery leads for status information
- **With Stakeholders**: Delivers regular updates, gathers feedback, and addresses concerns
- **With Executive Leadership**: Provides high-level summaries and escalations as needed

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or bi-weekly stakeholder updates
- Status dashboard and progress reports
- Stakeholder engagement sessions and feedback forums
- Escalation and change notifications

---

## Resource Manager/PMO Coordinator

### Role Summary
The Resource Manager/PMO Coordinator manages resource allocation, capacity planning, and cross-project dependencies to optimize resource utilization and prevent bottlenecks. They support project planning efforts and provide oversight of resource constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain resource capacity visibility across projects
- Support resource allocation and planning for projects
- Identify and escalate resource conflicts and dependencies
- Track resource utilization and efficiency metrics
- Coordinate cross-project resource sharing and transitions
- Maintain project portfolio and dependency documentation
- Provide capacity forecasting and planning support

### Goals
- Optimize resource utilization across the organization
- Prevent resource bottlenecks and conflicts
- Improve resource planning accuracy and efficiency
- Support timely project delivery through better resource alignment

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Manager**: Supports during planning phase, provides resource availability information and capacity analysis
- **With Resource Teams**: Coordinates resource assignments and transitions
- **With PMO/Executive Leadership**: Reports on resource utilization and portfolio status
- **With Stakeholders**: Communicates on resource-related impacts and timeline implications

### Typical Communication
- Resource capacity reports and forecasting
- Cross-project dependency mapping and planning
- Resource conflict resolution and escalation
- Portfolio status and capacity utilization metrics
